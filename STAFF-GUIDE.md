# Staff guide: how to post a notice in 2 minutes

For school office staff. You only need a web browser. You cannot break the website.

**Studio address:** https://veernewlook.sanity.studio
(Ask KeshavCo for an invitation the first time. Log in with your Google account or email.)

---

## Post a notice or CBSE circular

1. Open the Studio and click **Notices and circulars** on the left.
   `[SCREENSHOT: Studio home with "Notices and circulars" highlighted]`
2. Click the **pencil / + icon** (top of the list) to start a new notice.
   `[SCREENSHOT: new notice button]`
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: short and clear, e.g. *Half-yearly exam timetable, Classes 6 to 10*.
   - **Web address**: click **Generate**.
   - **Category**: CBSE Circular, School Notice, Event, Result or Holiday.
   - **Date**: today is filled in for you.
   - **Summary**: one or two sentences parents will see on the board (200 characters at most).
   `[SCREENSHOT: filled-in form]`
4. Optional:
   - **Full notice**: longer text, with bold, lists and links.
   - **PDF attachment**: drag the PDF in.
   - **Official link**: for CBSE circulars, paste the link from **cbse.gov.in** or **cbseacademic.nic.in**.
     (Please always link to the official CBSE page rather than copying the circular.)
   - **Pin to the top of the board**: for very important notices.
   - **Take off the board on**: the notice moves to the archive after this date. Nothing is ever deleted.
5. Click **Publish** (bottom right). Done.
   `[SCREENSHOT: Publish button]`

**When does it appear?** Within 10 minutes on the website, the Notice Board, and the home-page ticker.
Notices dated within the last 7 days show a red **New** tag automatically.

**Sharing:** every notice has its own page with **WhatsApp** and **Copy link** buttons. Share that link in class groups.

---

## Post the monthly newsletter

1. Click **Newsletters**, then **+**.
2. Title, click **Generate** for the web address, pick the **Month**.
3. Add the **Newsletter PDF**, a **Cover image** and a short **Intro**.
4. **Publish**.

## Add photos of an event

1. Click **Photo albums**, then **+**.
2. Event name, **Generate**, event date, cover photo.
3. Drag up to 40 photos into **Photos**. For each photo, fill in **What is in the photo?** in a few words.
   Please **never write children's full names**.
4. YouTube videos: paste the YouTube links (optional).
5. **Publish**.

## Change the admission session or show an emergency message

Click **Site settings**:
- **Admission session**: e.g. `2027-28`. The home page then says "Admissions open for 2027-28".
- **Emergency banner**: a red message on every page, e.g. *School closed tomorrow due to heavy rain.*
  **Remember to clear it** (delete the text and Publish) when it is no longer needed.
- **Show the notice ticker**: turn the scrolling notices on the home page on or off.

---

## Fix a mistake

Open the notice, correct it, click **Publish** again. To take a notice down at once, set
**Take off the board on** to yesterday's date and Publish, or use the **⋯ menu → Unpublish**.
